It's a real JEE Main 2024 PYQ (31 January, Shift 1): the limit as x goes to 0 of (e^(2|sin x|) - 2|sin x| - 1)/x^2. L'Hopital works, but you'd do it twice and handle both sides of 0.

You don't have to. Let t = 2|sin x|. Near 0, e^t is 1 + t + t^2/2, so the top is just t^2/2 = 2 sin^2 x. Divide by x^2 and the limit is 2.
