A maths puzzle for JEE Main prep: a rope hugs the Earth all the way round. Add 1 metre to it. How high does it lift?

You probably guessed a hair or a millimetre. It's about 16 cm, enough to slide your phone under.

The circumference is 2 pi r. Make it 1 metre longer and 2 pi (r + h) = 2 pi r + 1, so h = 1/(2 pi), about 0.16 m. The r cancels. The Earth, a football or a coin: same 16 cm gap.
