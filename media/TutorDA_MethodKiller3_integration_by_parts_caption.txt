∫ x²eˣ dx is the classic integration by parts question in JEE Main maths. The school way is two rounds: u = x², then u = 2x, with signs to track each time.

Here's the shortcut. Keep eˣ outside and write the polynomial. Subtract its derivative, add the next one, and stop when you hit 0: x² − 2x + 2. So ∫ x²eˣ dx = eˣ(x² − 2x + 2) + C.

The rule: for any polynomial P times eˣ, the answer is eˣ(P − P′ + P″ − …) + C. Differentiate it and you land back on x²eˣ.
