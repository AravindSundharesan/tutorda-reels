JEE Main marking scheme and negative marking: +4 right, -1 wrong, 0 blank. Should you guess an MCQ?

Do the expected value. A blind guess is right 1 time in 4, so on average it's (4 - 1 - 1 - 1)/4 = +0.25 marks. Cut one wrong option and it's (4 - 1 - 1)/3, about +0.67. Cut two and it's (4 - 1)/2 = +1.5.

It's an average, not a promise, and it's for the MCQ section. Still, skipping always scores 0.
