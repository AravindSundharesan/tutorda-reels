The question: the sum from n = 1 to 15 of 1/(n(n+1)) - 1/((n+2)(n+3)).

You don't need all 30 fractions. Call T(n) = 1/(n(n+1)) and the second part's just T(n+2), the same list shifted by 2. It's a telescoping sum, so 13 pairs cancel and you're left with 4 terms: T1 + T2 = 2/3, T16 + T17 = 1/144. 2/3 - 1/144 = 95/144. Option B.
