The question: at what angle of projection are the horizontal range and the maximum height equal?

You don't need both formulas. For any projectile, R = 4H / tan(theta). Set R = H and tan(theta) = 4, so the angle is tan^-1(4), about 76 degrees. Option A. Option B, tan^-1(1/4), is the same idea flipped upside down.
