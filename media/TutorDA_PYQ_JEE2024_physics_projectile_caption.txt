JEE Main 2024 PYQ (8 Apr, Shift 2), physics: projectile motion. Most students say 45. It's not.

The question: at what angle of projection are the horizontal range and the maximum height equal?

Skip the two formulas. For any projectile, the range is R = 4H / tan(theta), so when R = H you get tan(theta) = 4. That's tan^-1(4), about 76 degrees. Option A. B is the trap: the same answer flipped.
