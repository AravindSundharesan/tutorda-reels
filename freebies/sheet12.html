12 JEE maths tricks
that skip the long algebra

Every one is from a TutorDA reel. Learn the idea once, use it on a whole family of questions.

  1. How many solutions does sin x = x/10 have?
    sin x stays in [−1, 1], so only −10 ≤ x ≤ 10 matters. Sketch both graphs and count crossings.
    Answer: 7
  2. How many solutions does |x| = cos x have?
    cos x ≤ 1, so |x| ≤ 1. One crossing on the right, and the graphs are symmetric.
    Answer: 2 (x ≈ ±0.739)
  3. Which is bigger, 2100 or 363?
    Compare logs, not numbers: 100 log 2 = 30.10 and 63 log 3 = 30.06.
    Answer: 2100 (both have 31 digits)
  4. Which is bigger, eπ or πe?
    Take ln and divide by πe: compare ln x / x at e and π. It peaks at x = e.
    Answer: eπ (23.14 > 22.46)
  5. How many real roots does x3 − 3x + 1 = 0 have?
    f′ = 0 at x = ±1. f(−1) = 3 is above the axis, f(1) = −1 is below: up, down, up.
    Answer: 3 real roots
  6. ∫01 x(1 − x)99 dx = ?
    Replace x by 1 − x (a + b − x property): ∫01 (x99 − x100) dx.
    Answer: 1/100 − 1/101 = 1/10100
  7. What does sin x / x approach as x → 0?
    Areas on the unit circle give sin x < x < tan x, so cos x < sin x / x < 1.
    Answer: 1 (squeezed)
  8. What fraction of the unit square lies under y = x2?
    Rectangle sums close in on ∫01 x2 dx. Not half: the curve hugs the axis.
    Answer: 1/3
  9. 1 + 3 + 5 + … + (2n − 1) = ?
    Each odd number wraps one more L-shape around a square of dots.
    Answer: n2
  10. Two dice: which total comes up most?
    Draw the 6 × 6 grid of 36 pairs. Totals of 7 fill the whole diagonal.
    Answer: 7, with probability 6/36 = 1/6
  11. Biggest rectangle inside a circle of radius r?
    Corner at angle θ: area = 4r2 sin θ cos θ = 2r2 sin 2θ, largest at θ = 45°.
    Answer: a square, area 2r2
  12. Monty Hall: after a goat door opens, switch?
    Your first pick wins 1 time in 3. The other 2/3 moves to the last closed door.
    Answer: switch, win 2/3 of the time
