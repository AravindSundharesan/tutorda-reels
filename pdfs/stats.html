10 JEE Main statistics questions

Real past questions on mean, variance and standard deviation, easiest first. Answers and worked solutions on the last pages. Try them first.

Rules you'll use
  • Add a constant to every value (x + c): the mean moves by c, the variance and σ don't change.
  • Multiply every value by k: σ is multiplied by |k|, the variance by k².
  • σ² = Σx²n − x̄². Variance of 1, 2, …, n = n² − 112.

Answers and solutions
1 (B) variance.
Adding 10 to every mark moves the mean, median and mode up by 10. The gaps between marks stay the same, so the variance doesn't change.
2 18
Variance of 1, …, n = (n² − 1)/12 = 10, so n = 11. The first m even numbers are 2 × (1, …, m), so their variance is 4(m² − 1)/12 = 16, giving m = 7. m + n = 18.
3 3
An A.P. is b₁ + d·(0, 1, …, 10). Adding b₁ changes nothing; multiplying by d multiplies the variance by d². Variance of 0, …, 10 is (11² − 1)/12 = 10, so 10d² = 90 and d = 3.
4 (B) −7
Variance of 1, …, 17 is (17² − 1)/12 = 24 and its mean is 9. For ax + b: 24a² = 216, so a = 3; 9a + b = 17, so b = −10. a + b = −7.
5 (A) 425
The original mean is 0 and the SD is |a|. Adding b makes the mean b = 5 and leaves the SD at |a| = 20. a² + b² = 400 + 25 = 425.
6 (C) 10/√3
Six-test total = 6 × 48 = 288, so the sixth mark is 288 − 240 = 48. Deviations from 48: −3, 6, −7, 9, −5, 0; squares add to 200. Variance = 200/6 = 100/3, so SD = 10/√3.
7 (C) √3.96
Σx = 200 and Σx² = 20 × (4 + 100) = 2080. Correcting 8 to 12: Σx = 204, Σx² = 2080 − 64 + 144 = 2160. Variance = 2160/20 − (10.2)² = 108 − 104.04 = 3.96.
8 6344
Mean 56 gives α + β = 560 − 448 = 112. The known eight values have Σx² = 25678, and (α² + β² + 25678)/10 − 56² = 66.2 gives α² + β² = 6344 (α = 62, β = 50).
9 29
Σf = 22, Σfx = 176, so the mean is 8. Σfx² = 2048. Variance = 2048/22 − 64 = 320/11 ≈ 29.09, so [k] = 29.
10 (A) 2
Σx = 10 × 6/5 = 12, so 12 − 10α = 2 and α = 1. Variance is unchanged by shifting, so 84/25 = 40/10 − (6/5 − β)², giving (6/5 − β)² = 16/25 and β = 2 (β = 2/5 isn't an integer). β/α = 2.
